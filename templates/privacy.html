Privacy & your data

What we store

Your account (name, email), membership, class bookings, workouts you log, 1-to-1 requests and payments — all kept inside this app only, to run your membership. We don't sell or share it, and we don't use it for anything beyond providing the service.

The AI coach

The AI feature is powered by Groq (a third-party processor). We only send it your question and — only if you opt in — anonymous activity totals (e.g. number of classes and workouts). We never send your name, email, username, or your raw logs. Conversations are not stored — each question is processed once and then gone.

AI personalization is {{ 'available' if ai_enabled else 'currently not configured' }}. It is off by default and you choose when to enable it.

Anonymous feedback

Feedback you submit is stored with no link to your account — we cannot tell who wrote it, by design.

Your rights (GDPR)

  • Access / portability — ask us for a copy of your data.
  • Rectification — ask us to correct anything wrong.
  • Erasure — delete everything, right now, below.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off AI personalization anytime.

Delete my data

This permanently erases your account and all linked data (bookings, workouts, sessions, reminders, payments). This cannot be undone.
